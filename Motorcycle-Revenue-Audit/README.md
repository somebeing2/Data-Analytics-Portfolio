 Wholesale Revenue Audit: Motorcycle Parts
Domain: Supply Chain & Business Operations

 Context
The Board of Directors required a granular analysis of wholesale revenue to understand how profitability fluctuates across regional warehouses and product lines.

 Key SQL Implementations
- Net Revenue Logic: Used `(SUM(total) - SUM(payment_fee))` to isolate actual profit margins.
- Temporal Analysis: Extracted and formatted month names from raw dates to identify seasonal trends.
- Operational Filtering: Strictly segmented 'Wholesale' client types to provide high-level B2B insights.

  Executive Summary
- Top Performing Warehouse: Central Warehouse
- Key Finding: Wholesale revenue was audited across June, July, and August to confirm margin stability.
